Achiote / About

Food-memory forensics, built for families and agents.

Achiote exists because a lost dish or drink rarely starts with a clean recipe name. It starts with a smell, a texture, a sound-alike word, a person, and a lot of uncertainty.

Who is behind it

Achiote is published by Kyanite Labs and maintained as a source-available Model Context Protocol server plus hosted food-memory app.

Contact: support@kyanitelabs.tech.

What we protect

We separate what the user said, what research supports, what the model inferred, and what remains unknown. That evidence boundary is the product.

Why Achiote exists

Generic AI can be fluent too early. Recipe search assumes you already know the name. Achiote slows the answer down long enough to ask better questions, preserve provenance, and test the smallest bite or sip before anyone spends money or cooks a full dish.
