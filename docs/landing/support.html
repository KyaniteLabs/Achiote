Achiote / Support

Support

Email: support@kyanitelabs.tech

Response target: within 2 business days during launch. Security and payment-impacting issues are prioritized.

What to include

For bugs, include the page, approximate time, browser/device, visible error, and whether you were using an API key or demo password. Do not send card numbers, raw API keys, or sensitive family details unless needed.

Refund, cancellation, and billing help

Stripe billing is not live until pricing keys are configured. After billing is live, use the billing portal when available or email support with "refund", "cancel", or "billing help" in the subject.

Data requests

For privacy export, correction, or deletion requests, email support with "Achiote data request" in the subject. We will verify ownership before acting.

Security reports

For vulnerabilities, email support and include reproduction steps, affected endpoint, and impact. Please do not publicly disclose before we have a chance to investigate.
