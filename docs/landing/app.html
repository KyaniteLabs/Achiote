What's the dish you can almost remember?

Describe a smell, a texture, a nickname, or the person who made it. Spelling does not matter — half memories are enough. Any sourcing help is static sourcing guidance, not live inventory.

Start with any three clues. The answer gets better when you include one person/place clue, one sensory clue, and one uncertainty.

Who or whereWho made it, or where you ate it: abuela's kitchen, school lunch, a street cart, a holiday table.
Sensory triggerA smell, texture, color, wrapper, sauce, crunch, sour note, spice bloom, or serving temperature.
UncertaintyWhat you are unsure about: name, region, ingredient, filling, cooking method, or the closest sound-alike word.
