Achiote / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2026. This plain-language policy is for the hosted Achiote web app and API. Self-hosted deployments control their own data.

What Achiote collects

Achiote may process food memories, optional chat history, API keys, usage events, billing records after Stripe is configured, and support messages you send us. Food memories can include family, culture, place, and health-adjacent context, so we treat them as sensitive personal stories even when they are not legally classified that way.

What Achiote does not collect

We do not ask for card numbers directly. Payment details, when enabled, are handled by Stripe. We do not sell personal data. Telemetry is designed to count product events, not store prompt text.

Telemetry and logs

We collect privacy-preserving events such as page opened, ask started, ask succeeded, ask failed, and feedback category. We do not intentionally store full food-memory prompts in analytics events. Server logs may include operational metadata such as endpoint, status, provider/tool state, timestamps, and error class.

Retention

Operational logs should be retained for no more than 90 days unless needed for security, abuse prevention, accounting, or legal obligations. Support records may be kept while an issue is open and for a reasonable follow-up period. Billing records follow accounting and payment-provider requirements.

Data deletion

To request Data deletion, export, or correction, email support@kyanitelabs.tech with the subject "Achiote data request". Include the email or API key identifier connected to the request. We will verify ownership before deleting account-linked records.

Security

Use HTTPS, keep API keys private, and do not paste secrets into food-memory prompts. If you believe you found a vulnerability, follow the process in Support or the repository security policy.
