Why build a food memory detective?

Because family recipes are often remembered as fragments first: the pan, the smell, the aunt who made it, the word nobody can spell, the one texture that tells you the answer is close.

Food science

The science behind why one bite brings it back

Aroma, fat, browning, acid, sugar, starch texture, and serving temperature can carry more memory than a full ingredient list. Achiote tests those mechanisms before it asks anyone to cook.

Family research

How to interview your family about lost recipes

Ask for sensory clues, occasions, rough names, who made it, where ingredients came from, and what everyone disagrees about. Keep unknowns visible until someone checks them.
